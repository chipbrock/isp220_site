Example 2: The Total Energy Relation

The Question:

The relation of the total energy to momentum is not an obvious extrapolation from our original total energy relation. So let's work through this:

How do we go from

(1)ET=mγc2

to

(2)ET2=m2c4+p2c2 ?

The Answer:

Here's what we know:

energy: ET=mγc2(3)momentum: p=mγv

Let's square Equation 1:

(4)ET2=m2γ2c4

and look at the square of the gamma function...a kind of insider-trick among relativistic aficionados.

γ=11−β2γ2=11−β2

and use that in Equation 4:

(5)ET2=m2γ2c4(6)ET2=m2c411−β2multiply through the big fraction: (7)ET2(1−β2)=m2c4 almost there(8)ET2−ET2β2=m2c4(9)ET2=m2c4+ET2β2

Now look at the last term in Equation 9 and do some manipulation remembering the momentum equation and that β=v/c.

ET2β2=m2γ2c4β2(10)ET2β2=(mγc)2(c2β2)(11)ET2β2=(mγc)2v2(12)ET2β2=(mγv)2c2(13)ET2β2=p2c2

so now we have it by substituting Equation 12 into Equation 9:

(14)ET2=m2c4+ET2β2(15)ET2=m2c4+p2c2
